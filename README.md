# Financial Statement Analysis & Forecasting – Global FMCG Sector

## Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive financial statement analysis and forecasting study of four leading global FMCG companies operating in the beverages and snacks industry: **PepsiCo, The Coca-Cola Company, Keurig Dr Pepper, and Mondelez International**.

The analysis examines historical financial performance, key financial ratios, business trends, and projected financial statements to evaluate the companies' financial health, operating performance, growth potential, and sustainability.

## Project Objectives

- Analyse five years of financial performance from FY2019 to FY2023.
- Compare revenue, net income, total assets, and their growth trends.
- Evaluate liquidity, profitability, solvency, efficiency, and valuation ratios.
- Prepare financial projections for FY2024E–FY2026E using specified assumptions.
- Assess industry dynamics, competitive positioning, regulatory factors, and company-specific risks.
- Conduct SWOT analysis and compare the companies' financial performance and business sustainability.

## Key Areas of Analysis

### 1. Historical Financial Statement Analysis
- Revenue and net income trends
- Total asset growth
- Five-year financial performance comparison
- Compound Annual Growth Rate (CAGR) analysis

### 2. Financial Ratio Analysis
Comparative analysis of FY2023 financial ratios, including:

- **Profitability:** Gross Profit Margin, Net Profit Margin, Return on Assets (ROA), and Return on Equity (ROE)
- **Liquidity:** Current Ratio and Quick Ratio
- **Solvency:** Debt-to-Equity Ratio and Interest Coverage Ratio
- **Efficiency:** Asset Turnover Ratio
- **Valuation:** Price-to-Earnings (P/E) Ratio and Dividend Yield

### 3. Financial Forecasting
Prepared financial projections for FY2024E–FY2026E, covering:

- Revenue projections
- Net income projections
- Revenue growth assumptions
- Net profit margin assumptions
- Cost of goods sold and selling, general and administrative expenses
- Capital expenditure and tax assumptions

### 4. Non-Financial Analysis
- Global FMCG industry and competitive landscape
- Regulatory and economic factors
- Auditor reports and qualifications
- Management Discussion and Analysis (MD&A)
- Company-wise SWOT analysis
- Key risks and growth opportunities
- Sustainability of business models

## Key Findings

- **PepsiCo:** Recorded the highest FY2023 revenue among the four companies, supported by its diversified beverages and convenient foods portfolio.
- **Coca-Cola:** Reported the highest FY2023 gross profit margin and net profit margin, reflecting its asset-light franchise-based business model.
- **Keurig Dr Pepper:** Demonstrated revenue growth but had the highest debt-to-equity ratio among the companies analysed, highlighting the importance of leverage management.
- **Mondelez International:** Recorded the highest five-year revenue CAGR among the peer group, supported by pricing strategies and expansion in emerging markets.

## Data Sources

The analysis primarily uses publicly available audited annual reports (Form 10-K) for FY2019–FY2023, obtained through the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC EDGAR).

Supplementary sources include Macrotrends and Yahoo Finance.
